Summary:
Aditya Epoch is a modern college website with online admissions, contact form, testimonials, and admin submission review.

Key Features:
- Responsive mobile-friendly website
- Courses and placements information
- Online admission form and backend storage
- Contact form with backend messaging
- Admin login restricted for authorized users only
- Testimonials video gallery

Notes:
- The admin page is for authorized staff only.
- Visitors who are not authorized should not attempt to log in.
- New pages now open from the top of the page.
